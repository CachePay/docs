# 02 · MENA Competitors

> Access date: **2026-10-03**. Confidence: High = official, Medium = press/reviews, Low = inferred.
> Honest note: we found **no large MENA personal-finance app that is clearly live and marketing in Egypt today**. The MENA players below are either regional apps that list Egyptian banks, or apps that announced Egypt expansion plans.

---

## 1. Masrofi (مصروفي): strongest MENA feature set
| Item | Detail | Source / Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Country | Developer Mohammed H MS Arabi. The store page doesn't state a country; it **appears** Saudi/Gulf | [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1467616866) High (listing), country Low |
| Pricing | Free tier (3 accounts, 20 categories, 3-month history, 1 budget) · **Personal $4.99/month, $39.99/year** · **Business $9.99/month, $79.99/year** | High |
| Traction | 4.0★ from 2 ratings on the store we checked (small) | High |

**Complete verified features**
| Category | Features |
|---|---|
| Capture | Bank SMS import for **190+ banks across MENA** · receipt OCR · multi-account income and expense tracking |
| AI | **On-device AI assistant "Ask Masrofi"** that works offline |
| Planning | Savings goals · **net worth** · budget alerts · spending forecasts |
| Local / Islamic | **Zakat calculator with live gold prices** · **Jameya (rotating savings group) management** |
| Bills | Bills and subscriptions centre |
| Business | Invoicing, reports, PDF export · IRS tax mode for US users |
| Platform | 29 currencies · widgets · Siri · Spotlight |

- **Top features:** broad MENA SMS coverage, Jameya + Zakat (culturally native), and the offline AI.
- **Strengths:** the most "MENA-native" feature list we found. It's the only app in the region that **tracks gam'eyas**.
- **Weaknesses:** very few ratings, it isn't Egypt-specific (no Egyptian dialect voice found), the free tier is restrictive, and it has no family hierarchy.
- **Egypt gaps:** pricing is USD-based, with no evidence of Egyptian-dialect voice, cash/ATM sync or installment (valU/0%) tracking.

## 2. Wally / WallyGPT (UAE)
| Item | Detail | Source / Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Country | Dubai, UAE (moved to Bangalore in 2015 and later returned to DIFC). Largest market is the **US** | [Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/en/2023/12/evolution-wally-expenses-tracking-personal-finance-ai) Medium |
| Pricing | v3 "Gold" AED 40/month or AED 150/year. **v4 (WallyGPT) is free**, with monetisation planned via investment plug-ins | Medium |
| Coverage | "15,000 banks in 70 countries" via Plaid and Salt Edge · Lean Technologies partner (UAE) · SAMA sandbox (KSA) | Medium |

**Features:** expense tracking and categorisation · receipt scanning · bank sync · **WallyGPT AI finance advisor** · investment advice · credit-card suggestions · **groups for shared finances** · bill reminders · document sharing · group shopping lists · goals.

Sources: [Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/en/2023/12/evolution-wally-expenses-tracking-personal-finance-ai), [TechRadar review](https://www.techradar.com/reviews/wally-personal-finance). Medium.

- **Top features:** the AI chat advisor and bank sync across many countries.
- **Weaknesses:** it leans toward a global/US audience, and Egyptian bank sync isn't confirmed.
- **Egypt gaps:** Egypt has no open-banking aggregators at scale (our SRS rules out direct bank APIs for the same reason), so Wally's main capture method **doesn't work for most Egyptian users** (Low, inferred). We found no Egyptian Arabic voice and no cash wallet.

## 5. Hakbah (KSA): indirect, group savings
- **What:** a Saudi digital group-savings platform (a digitised jam'iya) with an "AI-powered savings engine". It has **500k+ users** and uses Tarabut open banking ([Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2024/01/219681-hakbah-a-fintech-savings-platform-partners-with-tarabut-menas-open-banking-provider/), Medium).
- **Relevance:** it's the regional equivalent of Money Fellows. It shows **group saving is a MENA-wide habit**, not just an Egyptian one.

## 6. Tarabut (Bahrain): enabler, not a competitor
- MENA's largest regulated open-banking platform ([Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2024/01/219681-hakbah-a-fintech-savings-platform-partners-with-tarabut-menas-open-banking-provider/), Medium).
- **Relevance:** if Egypt opens up to open banking in future, a provider like this would make Gulf-style bank-synced PFM apps possible in Egypt. That's a **medium-term threat to SMS-first products**, so our moat can't be capture alone.

---

## MENA takeaways
1. **Nobody combines** Egyptian SMS coverage + Egyptian-dialect voice + household roles + informal debt in one app.
2. **Masrofi** proves that culturally native features (Jameya, Zakat, gold) are buildable and wanted. It's the feature set to beat in the region.
3. Gulf PFM apps rely on **open banking**, which isn't available in Egypt yet. That's a structural opening for an SMS-plus-cash-first app.
