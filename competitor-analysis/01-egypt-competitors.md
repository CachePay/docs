## 1. Say: "Hands free budget app" ⭐ (the named competitor)

### Is this the right "Say"?
Several unrelated products share the name ("SayMoney" (an older iOS budget app), "Saye" (iOS budget planner), and a "Say" listing on a Japanese voice-AI directory that turned out to point to the same sayapp.net). We picked **Say – Hands free budget app (sayapp.net)** because:
- It's in **our exact domain**: bank-SMS + voice expense tracking, subscriptions, installments ([sayapp.net](https://www.sayapp.net/), High).
- Its website links to the **Egyptian App Store** ([sayapp.net](https://www.sayapp.net/), High).
- In-app prices on the store are in **EGP** (Pro EGP 99.99/month) ([App Store EG](https://apps.apple.com/eg/app/id6746735689), High).
- The reviewers are Egyptian and mention Egyptian investment apps (Thndr, "Menthum") ([App Store EG reviews](https://apps.apple.com/eg/app/id6746735689?see-all=reviews), Medium).

**Confidence that this is the right Say: High.**

### Top 3 reasons users choose Say
1. **Zero-typing capture** from bank SMS, plus voice for cash. Reviewers say it "automates data entry" and is "like having my own assistant" ([reviews](https://apps.apple.com/eg/app/id6746735689?see-all=reviews), Medium).
2. **Voice in Egyptian Arabic or mixed dialect.** It's the headline feature ("Hands free") ([store](https://apps.apple.com/eg/app/id6746735689), High).
3. **Clean, simple UX** at a local price (EGP 99.99/month) ([reviews](https://apps.apple.com/eg/app/id6746735689?see-all=reviews), Medium).

### Strengths
- It's the first mover in Egypt for SMS + voice, with real traction (2.6k ratings, 4.8★).
- It ships fast (v1.0.64 in about 15 months) and the developer replies to reviews.
- It already covers installments and credit cards, which shows it understands local needs.

### Weaknesses: real user complaints ([App Store EG reviews](https://apps.apple.com/eg/app/id6746735689?see-all=reviews), Medium)
| Complaint (paraphrased) | Reviewer / date | What it means for us |
|---|---|---|
| "ATM withdrawals need automatic cash account transfers", and ATM fees should be parsed from SMS | Ahmed AlMoselhy, 9 May | **Our US 2.7 (ATM → Cash Wallet) answers this directly** |
| All bank SMS go to the default account, so they have to move almost every transaction by hand | Bahraawy, 2 Aug | Routing exists but users don't discover it, which makes onboarding a weakness |
| Wants scheduled and installment payments, semi-annual billing, and "goals should be calculated as monthly budget" | ItsMoWaly, 24 Jul | Our goal ↔ budget links (US 3.9 rollover to goals) and installment ledger |
| Wants more than 3 accounts, plus debt and asset entries | 22lse, 1 Aug | Free tier is capped at 3 accounts, and there's **no debt/IOU ledger** |
| Supplementary card support | Kirolos Rezkalla, 18 Apr | Card hierarchy is a gap |
| Investment tracking (Thndr, funds, stocks, crypto, real estate) | Cherifefarid, Anas S, ItsMoWaly | Asked for repeatedly, and still unmet |

### Gaps for the Egyptian user
- **No household or family features**: no partner sharing and no kid accounts (none found in any source; Low, absence-based).
- **No personal IOU or informal debt ledger** (users explicitly ask for "debt entries").
- **No savings goals engine** beyond goal widgets: no simulation, no goal health tracking.
- **No AI chat or "can I afford it?"** advisor (only weekly AI insights).
- No receipt OCR mentioned.
- The interface language on the store is listed as English only, so the Arabic UI is unclear (voice understands Arabic) ([store](https://apps.apple.com/eg/app/id6746735689), Medium).

## 2. Masareef (مصاريف)
| Item | Detail | Source / Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Country / dev | Egypt, indie developer (Soliman Soliman) | [mwm.ai listing](https://mwm.ai/apps/masareef-expense-tracker/6760121459) Medium |
| Pricing | Free | Medium |
| Traction | 5.0★ from 2 ratings (very early) | Medium |
| Target users | Privacy-minded Egyptians and Gulf users | Low |

**Features**
- **Capture:** reads bank SMS and payment alerts on-device. It's claimed to recognise **130 banks, wallets and fintechs across Egypt, the Gulf and worldwide**.
- **Insights:** analytics by category, week and month · run-rate projections · monthly trends · merchant categorisation.
- **Budgets:** budget assistance · real-time progress indicators.
- **Bills:** recurring bill and subscription detection.
- **UX:** instant Arabic RTL ↔ English LTR switch.
- **Privacy:** offline-first, encrypted local storage, Face ID lock.

Sources: [mwm.ai](https://mwm.ai/apps/masareef-expense-tracker/6760121459), plus the search summary of [peerlist](https://peerlist.io/boogado66/project/masareef). Medium.

- **Top features:** offline/private SMS capture, and broad bank coverage.
- **Strengths:** free and private.
- **Weaknesses:** almost no traction, no voice, no family features, no debts.
- **Egypt gaps:** no cash wallet reconciliation, no installments.

## 3. Masareefy (مصاريفي)
| Item | Detail | Source / Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Dev | Omar Amin (indie, Egypt-targeted) | [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6779322746) High |
| Pricing | Manual logging free and unlimited · voice has a monthly free allowance · **Pro $1.99 / $14.99** · **Family $2.99/month, $19.99/year** | High |
| Traction | 1 rating (very early) | High |

**Features**
- **Capture:** voice-first in **Egyptian Arabic** ("fifty pounds on koshary, twenty on transport") · Apple Pay and bank SMS auto-logging · offline logging with later sync.
- **Budgets:** monthly and weekly budgets with donut charts.
- **Family:** **shared household or group accounts with multi-device sync**.
- **Recurring:** auto-logs rent, salary and subscriptions.
- **Privacy:** voice clips are deleted right after analysis · "nothing is saved until you approve it".
- **UX:** Arabic RTL and English · themes · emoji categories · reminder timing.

- **Top features:** Egyptian-dialect voice, and the **family plan**. It's the only Egyptian PFM app we found with household sharing.
- **Weaknesses:** tiny user base, no debts or installments, no AI advisor.
- **Egypt gaps:** no kid roles or permission hierarchy, only a flat shared account (inferred, Low).

## 6. Klivvr (indirect: family neobank)
- **Features (Go Plus, launched Sep 2024):** up to **5 cards per account** · per-card spending permissions and categories · **linked family members including minors**, managed and viewed by the primary holder · request and send money between family members · insights and history · double K-Points, cashback, 40+ merchants · lounge access · daily and monthly challenges.
- **Sources:** [Waya](https://waya.media/klivvr-introduces-family-oriented-go-plus-product-in-egypt/) Medium, and [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1586109111) High.
- **Why users pick it:** the family card hierarchy. The CEO calls it "a first in the Egyptian market".
- **Gap:** like Telda, it only sees its own cards. It has no budgets, cash, debts or goals beyond the card.

## 7. Money Fellows (indirect: digital gam'eya / ROSCA)
- **Features:** digital money circles of 6, 10 or 12 months with payouts of EGP 3,000–120,000 · choose your payout slot (fees up to 16% for early slots, 0% for late ones) · several circles at once · savings programme over 6–24 months (EGP 125/month up to EGP 1M payout) · pay via Fawry, banks, prepaid cards or wallets · reminders · Sharia-compliant fatwa · CBE regulatory sandbox.
- **Sources:** [Money Fellows guide](https://moneyfellows.com/en-us/blog-home/your-complete-guide-to-money-fellows-app/) High.
- **Traction:** **8.5M users** ([Daba Finance](https://dabafinance.com/fr/nouvelles/egypts-money-fellows-raises-13m-to-expand-digital-savings-platform) Medium).
- **Why it matters:** this proves that **informal group saving is mainstream** in Egypt. The gam'eyas people run with family and colleagues (outside the app) are still untracked by every PFM app in Egypt.

## 8. valU (indirect: BNPL / installments)
- **Features:** installments over 6–60 months at 3,500+ merchants · zero down payment and 0% offers · in-app view of all installments and monthly amounts · request tracking · help centre.
- **Traction:** **902k app users, 23% market share (Dec 2025)**.
- **Sources:** [AUC valU FAQ](https://business.aucegypt.edu/valu-faqs) Medium; [valU FY25 investor presentation](https://www.valu.com.eg/pdfs/FY25%20Investor%20Presentation.pdf) High.
- **Gap:** it only shows *its own* installments. Users who juggle valU, Sympl, bank 0% plans and card minimums have no single view, and that's **our Feature 9**.

## 9. InstaPay (indirect: payments rail)
- **Features:** send money by mobile number or IPA · collect/request money · bill payment · balance and mini statement (last 10 transactions) · transaction history.
- **Sources:** [PhotonPay guide](https://www.photonpay.com/hk/blog/article/instapay-egypt) Medium.
- **Scale:** 6.2M users (2023) and ~1.5B transactions worth EGP 2.9T in 2024 (same source, Medium).
- **Relevance:** InstaPay **SMS and notifications** are a key data source for us. Its "collect money" request is a natural pairing for an IOU reminder (we deep-link to it; we don't transfer money).

## 10. Splitwise, used in Egypt (indirect: IOUs)
This is a global product. See [03-global](03-global-competitors.md#splitwise). It matters in Egypt because the free tier now caps users at about 3 expenses per day ([Kimola report](https://kimola.com/reports/splitwise-app-feedback-report-uncover-user-insights-google-play-en-144452), Medium), so friend and family IOU tracking is underserved.

## Not verified
- **Bank apps (CIB, NBE, Banque Misr)** probably show categorised spending for their own cards, but we didn't verify this. Low.
- **Excel, notebooks and WhatsApp groups** are almost certainly the biggest real "competitor" for IOUs and gam'eyas. We have no source for this. Low.
- **Reddit (r/Egypt) sentiment** couldn't be collected because reddit.com blocks our crawler.
