## What the matrix tells us
- **We plan to have every Say feature except subscription "known services" breadth.** On paper we're a superset.
- **Features unique to us:** ATM → cash and daily cash check · salary-cycle budgets · goal simulator · penalty-first debt strategy · rewards calculator · approval-based AI actions · DND/rate-limited alerts · detach/graduate accounts.
- **Gaps in our plan that competitors fill:** gam'eya tracking (Masrofi), investments and net worth (Telda, Masrofi, requested by Say users), Zakat/gold (Masrofi), and a properly specified **IOU ledger** (Splitwise).
