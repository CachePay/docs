## 1. YNAB (You Need A Budget)
- **Target users:** people committed to a budgeting method.
- **Pricing:** $14.99/month or $109/year, with a 34-day trial ([unstar.app comparison](https://unstar.app/blog/ynab-rocket-money-monarch-everydollar-copilot-budget-apps-ranked-2026), Medium). The official pricing page wouldn't load for us.
- **Features:**
  - **Method:** zero-based budgeting ("give every dollar a job").
  - **Data:** bank sync and manual entry.
  - **Planning:** goals/targets.
  - **Platform:** web app (most complete) plus mobile apps.
  - Sources: [envelopebudgeting.com review](https://envelopebudgeting.com/articles/ynab-review) Medium; [pocketclear complaints](https://pocketclear.app/blog/ynab-complaints-problems.html) Medium.
- **Top feature:** the **method itself**: behaviour change and a community.
- **Complaints:** repeated price increases (legacy users roughly doubled) · learning curve longer than the trial · bank sync drops and duplicates · mobile apps missing web features ([pocketclear](https://pocketclear.app/blog/ynab-complaints-problems.html), Medium).

## 2. Monarch Money
- **Pricing:** $99.99/year with a 7-day trial ([unstar.app](https://unstar.app/blog/ynab-rocket-money-monarch-everydollar-copilot-budget-apps-ranked-2026); [Monarch pricing](https://www.monarch.com/pricing) High for the trial).
- **Features:**
  - **Data:** unlimited account sync (Plaid and Finicity).
  - **Budgets:** by category or group · cash-flow view.
  - **Wealth:** net worth · investments.
  - **Planning:** long-term planning.
  - **AI:** plain-English AI assistant.
  - **Household:** shared access for partners · "Needs Review" flag that assigns a transaction to a family member.
  - **Other:** reports · ad-free.
  - Sources: [Monarch pricing](https://www.monarch.com/pricing) High; [walletgrower](https://walletgrower.com/blog/monarch-vs-rocket-money-2026) Medium.
- **Top features:** household collaboration and the Mint replacement.
- **Complaints:** price, sync failures with some institutions, missing Mint features ([checkthat.ai](https://checkthat.ai/brands/monarch/reviews), [freenance](https://freenance.io/products/monarch-money-review-2026-mint-replacement-budget-app/), Medium).

## 3. Copilot Money
- **Pricing:** $95/year ($7.92/month), 1-month trial ([copilot.money](https://www.copilot.money/), High).
- **Platforms:** web, iPhone, iPad, Mac. **No Android** (High).
- **Features:**
  - **AI:** auto-tagging that learns.
  - **Budgets:** rollover budgets · daily "spending line".
  - **Analysis:** cash flow · subscription detection.
  - **Wealth:** net worth incl. crypto and real estate (Zillow) · investment tracking.
- **Top features:** polished design and accurate AI categorisation.
- **Gaps:** no Android at all (69% of Egypt) and no couples features mentioned.

## 4. Rocket Money
- **Pricing:** free tier; Premium $6–12/month, pay-what-you-want ([unstar.app](https://unstar.app/blog/ynab-rocket-money-monarch-everydollar-copilot-budget-apps-ranked-2026), Medium).
- **Features:**
  - **Subscriptions:** detection · **cancellation concierge (they cancel for you)**.
  - **Bills:** bill negotiation concierge.
  - **Alerts:** balance alerts.
  - **Tracking:** spend tracking · budgets (unlimited on Premium).
  - **Saving:** automated savings plan.
  - **Credit and wealth:** credit score monitoring · net worth.
  - **Household:** shared accounts.
  - Source: [rocketmoney.com](https://www.rocketmoney.com/), High.
- **Top feature:** finding and **cancelling** forgotten subscriptions.
- **Egypt gap:** the concierge model depends on US providers. Egyptian subscriptions (telecom bundles, gyms, streaming billed via carrier) can't be cancelled the same way.

## 5. Cleo
- **Pricing:** $5.99 or $14.99/month ([Penny Hoarder](https://www.thepennyhoarder.com/budgeting/cleo-app-review/), Medium).
- **Features:**
  - **AI chat:** chat-first coach · plain-English questions · **"roast me" mode**.
  - **Budgets:** auto-categorised, with category limits.
  - **Saving:** round-up savings wallet.
  - **Credit:** cash advance of $20–250 with no interest or credit check (subscription required).
  - Sources: [Penny Hoarder](https://www.thepennyhoarder.com/budgeting/cleo-app-review/), [tooliverse](https://tooliverse.ai/tools/cleo), Medium.
- **Top features:** **personality**, and conversational UX that appeals to Gen Z.
- **Lesson for us:** tone and humour drive engagement. An Egyptian-humour AI persona is an open opportunity.

## 6. Goodbudget
- **Pricing:**
  - **Free:** 20 envelopes, 2 devices, 1 year of history.
  - **Premium:** $10/month or $80/year. Unlimited envelopes, 5 devices, 7 years of history, bank sync.
  - Sources: [costbench](https://www.costbench.com/software/personal-finance/goodbudget/), [Experian](https://www.experian.com/blogs/ask-experian/goodbudget-budgeting-app-review/), Medium.
- **Features:** envelope budgeting · **household sharing across devices in real time** · debt tracking · reports.
- **Top feature:** a simple shared envelope system for couples.
- **Relevance:** Egyptians already budget with envelopes (cash). Goodbudget proves the model works, but it's manual and English-only.

## 7. Honeydue
- **Features:** shared balances · bills · shared budget · **splits between partners** · activity · **in-app chat with emoji reactions on transactions** · **customisable sharing (choose what to share)** · joint card waitlist.
- **Source:** [honeydue.com](https://www.honeydue.com/), High.
- **Top feature:** private-vs-shared control and the conversation inside the app.
- **Relevance:** it validates our partner toggle (US 1.3). The **chat and comment on transactions** feature is worth copying.

## 8. Splitwise
- **Pricing:** free tier limited to about **3 expenses per day with ads**; Pro ≈ $5/month, varying by region ([Kimola report](https://kimola.com/reports/splitwise-app-feedback-report-uncover-user-insights-google-play-en-144452), Medium).
- **Features:**
  - **Splitting:** split expenses · group and friend balances · debt simplification · itemisation.
  - **Pro:** receipt scanning · card transaction import · currency conversion · charts · search.
  - Source: [splitwise.com/pro](https://www.splitwise.com/pro), High.
- **Top feature:** "who owes whom", which has become a household name.
- **Complaints:** the daily limit, ads between entries, and basic features behind the paywall (Kimola, Medium).
- **Egypt gap:** no link to the user's own budget; an IOU never reduces your category budget. **Our US 3.8 plus an IOU ledger merges both.**

## 9. Greenlight
- **Pricing:** Core $5.99 · Max $10.98 · Infinity $15.98 · Family Shield $19.98 per month ([greenlight.com/plans](https://greenlight.com/plans), High).
- **Features:**
  - **Kids' money:** debit cards for up to 5 kids · chores, jobs and allowance · direct deposit · savings interest (2–6%) · 1% cash back.
  - **Learning and investing:** LevelUp financial literacy game · investing for kids and parents.
  - **Safety:** location sharing · SOS and crash detection · driving reports.
  - **Protection:** phone and purchase protection · identity and credit monitoring.
- **Top features:** **chores → allowance** and parental controls.
- **Relevance:** sets the bar for our kid sub-accounts. Without a card, we can still do **allowance, chores and goals** tracking.

## 10. Wallet by BudgetBakers
- **Features:** manual entry · receipt scanning · recurring rules · multi-currency · PSD2 bank sync · budgets with rollovers · **shared budgets** · planned payments with reminders · cash-flow forecasts.
- **Source:** [budgetbakers.com/llms.txt](https://budgetbakers.com/llms.txt), High.
- **Traction:** **11M+ users**.
- **Complaints:** bank connections that disconnect, and transaction discrepancies ([supermoney](https://www.supermoney.com/reviews/money-management/wallet), Medium).
- **Pricing:** not verified.

## 11. Money Manager (Realbyte)
- **Features:** manual ledger with **double-entry bookkeeping** · weekly, monthly and annual budgets · multiple accounts and currencies · scheduled transfers · quick-entry templates · calendar view · charts.
- **Source:** [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id560481810), High.
- **Traction:** 4.8★ from 284k+ ratings on iOS, 20M+ downloads on Android (search summary, Medium).
- **Top feature:** fast, reliable manual entry. It's popular with people who track cash.
- **Gap:** zero automation and no Arabic dialect.

## 12. axio (formerly Walnut), India
- **Model:** an RBI-registered NBFC. The free PFM funds a lending business ([APKMirror listing](https://www.apkmirror.com/apk/axio-digital-pvt-ltd/axio-expense-tracker-bnpl/), Medium).
- **Features:** **SMS-based spend detection** · budgets · bills and reminders · **Splits (group expenses)** · personal loans · BNPL "Checkout Finance" · fixed deposits.
- **Top feature:** automatic SMS tracking in a market that, like Egypt, has no open banking.
- **Lesson:** this is the **closest business-model analogue** for a free SMS-based PFM app in an emerging market. The PFM data is monetised through lending and marketplace partners. Relevant for our post-graduation business (no implementation implied).

## Global takeaways
- **Table stakes globally:** auto-capture, categorisation, budgets, subscriptions, reports, and an AI Q&A (Monarch, Cleo, Copilot).
- **What leaders charge:** about $95–110/year. That's far above Egyptian willingness to pay; Say charges ≈ EGP 700/year.
- **Household is a premium differentiator** (Monarch, Goodbudget, Honeydue, Greenlight), but **nobody combines partner + kids + IOUs** in one app.
- **Biggest global complaint:** sync breaking. SMS capture avoids aggregator outages, but brings its own parsing-accuracy risk.
